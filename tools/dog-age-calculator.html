Dog Age Calculator 🎂

The 7× rule is a myth, dogs sprint through their first two years, then age by size. Here's the real number.

Based on veterinary aging charts: ~15 human years in year one, ~9 in year two, then 4–7 per year by size. Every dog writes their own curve, this is the honest estimate.

Wondering what changes at each stage? The first-year guide covers the sprint, and senior dog care covers what to adjust when the muzzle goes grey.
