Dog truth #10

Dogs Can't Sweat It Out

Dog myth, busted
“He'll cool down like we do.”
Dogs cool by panting alone — flat faces barely manage that.

No sweat glands to speak of, a fur coat, and — for pugs, bulldogs and shih tzus — a shortened airway that vents heat poorly. What feels breezy to you in a t-shirt is genuinely hard work for them.

Flat-faced, senior and heavy-coated dogs sit one row stricter on every heat threshold: the limits.
