Dog truth #7

Plaque Hardens in 72 Hours

Dog myth, busted
“Dental chews have the teeth covered.”
Plaque turns to tartar in ~3 days, and no chew removes tartar.

By age three, most dogs have dental disease, and dental surgery under anaesthesia costs more than a lifetime of toothbrushes. Chews help; bristles at the gumline do the real work.

Three brushings a week is the honest minimum, the two-week no-drama ramp-up is in the brushing guide.
