A Wagging Tail Doesn't Mean Happy

Dog myth, busted
“The tail was wagging, so he was friendly!”
A wag means arousal. The mood is in the height and speed.

Mid-height sweeping wag with loose hips: genuinely friendly. High, stiff, fast tip-vibration: on alert — not an invitation. Low and slow: unsure. Tucked: scared.

Dogs wag before play, before fear, and occasionally before a bite. Read the whole dog — the full vocabulary is here.
