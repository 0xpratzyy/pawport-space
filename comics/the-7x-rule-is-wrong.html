Dog truth #5

The 7× Dog Years Rule Is Wrong

Dog myth, busted
“One dog year = seven human years.”
Year one ≈ 15 human years. Then it depends on size.

Dogs sprint through adolescence: about 15 human years in year one, 9 more in year two — then 4–7 per year, small dogs slowest and giant breeds fastest. A chihuahua and a Great Dane age on completely different clocks.
