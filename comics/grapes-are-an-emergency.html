Dog truth #4

Grapes Are a Vet-Now Emergency

Dog myth, busted
“It was just one grape…”
Grapes & raisins: no known safe dose. Call the vet, even for one.

Grape toxicity causes kidney failure and is unpredictable per dog, one dog shrugs off a bunch, another is in trouble from two raisins. There is no established safe amount, which is why every vet says the same thing: call now, not tomorrow.

Same emergency tier: xylitol (sugar-free gum, some peanut butters). The full danger-ranked list tells you what's a crisis and what's merely a bad idea.

One minute of science

Grapes and raisins cause acute kidney failure in some dogs at doses as small as a few grapes, and there is no way to know in advance which dogs are sensitive. Tartaric acid is the suspected culprit. Treat any amount as an emergency: call the vet immediately, do not wait for symptoms, which can take 24 hours. The full list is in what dogs can't eat.
