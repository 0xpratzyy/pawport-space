Dog truth #3

Sniffing IS the Walk

Dog myth, busted
“Stop sniffing, we're here to WALK.”
15 minutes of sniffing tires a dog like 45 minutes of walking.

A dog's nose has ~300 million scent receptors to our 6 million. That lamppost is their group chat, newspaper and gossip column, and reading it is intense cognitive work.

Nose-work measurably lowers pulse rates and produces the same happy exhaustion as much longer walks. Schedule the sniff: why the sniffari wins.
