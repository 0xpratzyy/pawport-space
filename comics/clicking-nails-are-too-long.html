Dog truth #6

Clicking Nails Are Too Long

Dog myth, busted
“The floor-clicking is just how dogs sound.”
Click-click on the kitchen floor = time for a trim.

Standing relaxed, nails shouldn't touch the ground. Overgrown nails change how the paw lands, push toes sideways, and quietly strain joints over months — it's posture, not cosmetics.

Most dogs need a trim every 3–4 weeks. The no-wrestling method (and the black-nail trick) is in the nail guide.
